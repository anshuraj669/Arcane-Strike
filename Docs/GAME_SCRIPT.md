# ARCANE STRIKE — Chapter 1: The Awakening

**Game script, as the game plays now** (written from the code on 3 Nov 2026)

Scene order: **Attic → Ascension Temple → Monster World**. Playtime is about 8–10 minutes.

How to read it:
- **PLAYER** is what the player does with their hands and body.
- **EMBER** is a spoken line. A line marked **(voiced)** has a recorded clip in `Resources/VO/Ember`, plays with no subtitle, and is used in the Ascension only. Every other line shows as a subtitle with Ember's crackle voice.
- **CARD** is text that fills the screen.

---

## SCENE 1 — THE OLD HOUSE (`Attic.unity`)

### 1.1 Midnight, outside

*Fade in from black. It's midnight in the yard of an old half-timbered house with a terracotta roof. Low stone walls, a well, a family graveyard, dead trees and a lamp post. Wind.*

*The view walks slowly and comfortably: up the porch, through the front door as it swings open, across the hall and up the stairs to the loft. The two doors of the old castle gate swing inward.*

> Skip: Space, or B / Y on a controller, jumps straight to the store room.

### 1.2 The store room

*An attic full of dust and junk nobody has touched in decades: a cracked gramophone, a dead television on a crate, sagging bookcases, burlap sacks, mouldy cardboard boxes, a knocked-over chair, dust sheets and cobwebs. A candle burns on a barrel. Moonlight comes through the window.*

*In the middle of the floor, an old chest is **shaking** by itself.*

From here on the player can walk freely.

| # | Beat | PLAYER | What happens |
|---|------|--------|--------------|
| 1 | **The chest** | Reaches to the front of the lid and lifts it | The lid creaks open and dust bursts out |
| 2 | **The lantern** | Closes a fist round the lantern's top ring | Grandfather's broken brass lantern comes up out of the chest, filthy |
| 3 | **Cleaning** | Picks up the old cloth from the chest with the other hand and rubs the lantern | Dust falls away, a soft hum starts, then a golden glow; runes light along its sides, and the hum grows louder |
| 4 | **The cut** | Touches the glinting sharp edge with a fingertip | It cuts the finger. If the player never finds the edge, the lantern cuts them anyway |
| 5 | **Blood** | — | A drop of blood touches the lantern and the wick lights by itself |

### 1.3 The awakening

*A burst of blue energy. Whispers fill the room. The books lift off the shelves and float. The attic shakes. Golden light wraps around the player, then the runes wake and blue light floods the room.*

*Space and time **tear open** in front of the player: a rift. It swallows the lantern, then pulls the player in.*

*Cut to white.*

---

## SCENE 2 — THE ASCENSION TEMPLE (`AscensionCave.unity`)

### 2.1 Arrival

*The white clears. The player stands in an open temple: a ring of pointed arches over still jade water, a golden sky, and a sun mosaic on the floor. At the centre, on a low gilded step, stands an ancient **bronze three-legged cauldron**, with **blue fire** rising tall from its mouth.*

*The lantern drifts over and hangs beside the cauldron. As the player walks closer, it glows brighter and hums louder.*

**PLAYER:** Walks into the light and touches the cauldron's rim.

### 2.2 The touch

*A shockwave rings out of the cauldron. Blue lightning cracks across the floor in every direction, the blue fire roars up, and a deep boom sounds while the hands rumble. (The view itself never shakes.)*

### 2.3 The Ascension

*A ring of blue fire rises from the floor around the player. Above, the sky splits open. A column of fire takes the player and lifts them off the floor.*

*The fire fills the view. Behind it, the **soul slips out of the body**. As the fire thins, the player is the soul, floating, and their own body hangs in the flames in front of them.*

*The lantern's flame comes loose and drifts out: **EMBER**, a living spark of fire. It finds the soul.*

> **EMBER** (voiced): It's been many years since someone has been here. You have no idea...
>
> **EMBER** (voiced): ...Right. Introductions.
>
> **EMBER** (voiced): Hii! My name is Ember.
>
> **EMBER** (voiced): And you...

*Ember stops and looks closer.*

> **EMBER** (voiced): Wait a minute. Oh. Oh, my, my... You have it.
>
> **EMBER** (voiced): Look at your hands. Go on... hold them up.

**PLAYER:** Raises both palms towards their face.

*A heartbeat, lub-dub. Golden mandalas burn into both palms: the **Jyoti Seal**.*

> **EMBER** (voiced): I don't believe it... the Jyoti Seal.
>
> **EMBER** (voiced): Do you have any idea what this means, kid? You aren't just a tourist. You're a **Weaver**.

### 2.4 The suit-up

*Ember moves aside. The ring of fire **parts towards the soul**, so nothing hides the body. The fire on the body dies down.*

*First a **sword forms out of the light**: a broad straight short sword with a bronze hilt and a red-wrapped grip. It turns and slides into a sheath on the back. Then the Weaver's outfit **wraps outward from the chest**, piece by piece. Blue sparks and lightning answer each piece, the gauntlets close over the hands, and the eyes light.*

### 2.5 Return

*The soul is drawn back into the body and the fire swallows the view. When it clears, the player is standing on the temple floor, **suited, armed, and wearing gauntlets**, a few steps away from the cauldron. The fire sinks.*

### 2.6 Sword practice (optional)

> **EMBER:** Before we go... do you want to try that blade first? I can make you a shadow to practise on.
>
> **EMBER:** Touch the blue stone on the left to train with me, or the orange one on the right to go straight on.

*Two glowing stone tablets hover within arm's reach: **TRAIN WITH EMBER** and **ONWARD TO THE MONSTER WORLD**.*

**If ONWARD:**

> **EMBER:** Straight on, then. I like your nerve.

**If TRAIN:**

> **EMBER:** Good. First, draw it: reach over your right shoulder and grip the hilt.

**PLAYER:** Reaches over the right shoulder and draws the sword.

*A shadow-figure made of smoke forms in front of the player.*

> **EMBER:** There. A shadow of smoke. It can't hurt you, and it can't die: cut it as often as you like.

Each lesson ends when the move is done. The ONWARD stone stays beside the player, so they can stop at any time.

| # | Lesson | EMBER says |
|---|--------|------------|
| 1 | SLASH | Cut at it. Swing fast: the faster the blade moves, the deeper it bites. |
| 2 | CROSS CUT | Now cut, and cut straight back the other way. A cross cut. |
| 3 | THRUST | Drive the point straight at it, along the line of the blade. |
| 4 | RISING SLASH | Drop the blade low, by your hip... then cut upward. |
| 5 | CLEAVE | Hold the blade flat and sweep it across in front of you. |
| 6 | LUNGE | A thrust so fast your whole body follows it in. |
| 7 | OVERHEAD SMASH | Lift the sword above your head... and bring it down hard. |
| 8 | WHIRLWIND | Sweep it all the way round you, as fast as you can. |
| 9 | JYOTI WAVE | Hold the blade upright and still until the light gathers in it... then swing. |
| 10 | PARRY | Now it fights back. When it rears up, hold your blade across in front of you. |
| 11 | DUCK | This one swipes high, at your head. Duck under it! |
| 12 | JUMP | And this one sweeps low. Jump over it! |
| 13 | BREAK ITS GUARD | See it hiding behind its arms? Slashes just bounce off. Thrust, smash, or cut up through it. |
| 14 | DEFLECT | It throws shadow fire. Swing through it as it comes, and send it straight back. |

> **EMBER:** That's everything I know. The rest you'll learn out there.
> *(or, if the player stopped early)* **EMBER:** Enough for now? Then let's go.

*The shadow drifts apart into smoke, and the blade goes back on the back.*

### 2.7 The way out

> **EMBER:** ...Do you hear that? The temple felt the Weaver wake.

*The temple rumbles. The **sealed gate** at the back breaks open, and beyond it a bridge leads over the water to a shrine glowing with another world's light.*

> **EMBER:** There's your way out. But that bridge doesn't lead home...
>
> **EMBER:** It leads into their world. The Kalakh's world. Bare rock and dead moonlight, as far as you can see.
>
> **EMBER:** They'll come for that blade. Better we meet them out there than let them in here. Follow the light, Weaver.
>
> **EMBER:** Walk on across the bridge... or stand still a moment, and I'll lead you there.

**PLAYER:** Walks across the bridge, or stands still and lets Ember lead them.

> **EMBER:** This way. Stay close to me.

*Through the shrine's threshold. Fade.*

## SCENE 3 — THE MONSTER WORLD (`MonsterWorld.unity`)

### 3.1 Arrival

*Fade in on a basalt ledge in the Kalakh's world. There's bare rock and jagged mountains, and one crimson peak straight ahead under a huge pink moon in a sky of stars and nebulae. Old warriors' skeletons and bones lie among the rocks, and flying beasts circle far off.*

> **EMBER:** We're through. This is their world, Weaver: the Kalakh's world.
>
> **EMBER:** Bare rock, a dead moon, and not one honest light in it. Nothing here has seen true light in a thousand years.
>
> **EMBER:** Draw your blade, Weaver. Reach over your right shoulder for the hilt.

**PLAYER:** Draws the sword.

> **EMBER:** Good. Stay close to the light, and don't let them get behind you.

### 3.2 The hunt

The **Ashhorn** soldiers have stag skulls, antlers, long arms and red cloth. There are **15 of them, in waves of 1, 2, 4 and 8**. Each soldier leaps in over the edge of the ledge from a random direction. **No two soldiers share an attack**, and which attack comes next is random. The next wave comes once everyone in the current wave is down to 20% health or dead.

The screen shows "Kalakh left N/15" and the health of each soldier.

| Wave | EMBER |
|------|-------|
| 1 (one soldier) | Something's coming... over the edge! Hold your blade across to parry, then cut it while it reels. |
| 2 (two) | Two more! They won't fight the same way, so watch each one. |
| 3 (four) | Four! Keep turning: they'll come from anywhere. |
| 4 (eight) | Eight of them! Don't let them get behind you, Weaver! |

The player fights with the sword moves from practice: slashes and combos, thrusts, guard breaks, parries, deflecting shadow fire, and leaning, ducking or jumping to dodge.

### 3.3 The Warlord

*When all fifteen are dead, a boulder bursts apart. The **Kalakh Warlord**, a crimson demon with crescent horns, leaps towards the player, lands in a crouch and roars in their face.*

> **EMBER:** That's... their Warlord. Every one of those soldiers learned from it.
>
> **EMBER:** Wear it down, then finish it: when it reels, drive the blade home.

The Warlord fights with eruptions, breath attacks, spins and berserk rushes.

### 3.4a Victory

> **EMBER:** It's down. The Warlord is down!

*The ground rumbles, and a roar answers from far across the world.*

> **EMBER:** Hear that? This whole world felt it. Every Kalakh in this world knows your name now, Weaver.

*Fade to black.*

> **CARD:** CHAPTER 1 COMPLETE — *The Awakening* — Prize: the Jyoti Blade
>
> **EMBER:** That blade is yours now. You earned it.

### 3.4b Knocked down (health reaches zero, but the fight can go on)

*The view goes dark red.*

> **EMBER:** Get up, Weaver! The light doesn't give up, and neither do you!

*Ember bursts with light, the Kalakh are thrown back and stagger, and the player is back on their feet.*

### 3.4c Fallen

*The world slows down. The Kalakh roar in triumph over the player, and the light goes out.*

> **CARD:** YOU HAVE FALLEN
>
> **EMBER:** Weaver! No... stay with me!
>
> **EMBER:** The light isn't done with you. I'll take you back to the temple. Rise again, and this time we win.

*The player wakes back in the temple, still suited and armed. There's no second Ascension.*

> **EMBER:** There you are. Breathe, Weaver... you're back in the temple. The light carried you home.
>
> **EMBER:** They knocked you down. They didn't put you out. Your blade's still on your back.

*Ember offers the practice stones again, then:*

> **EMBER:** The bridge is waiting. Let's break the way open again.
>
> **EMBER:** Back into their world. This time, watch their wind-ups, and watch your back.

*The player returns to the Monster World and the hunt starts over.*

- **Voice:** only the eight Ascension lines are recorded. Every other line is a subtitle with the crackle voice, so record those to give the whole chapter one voice.
- **Video (under 3 minutes):** good moments to show are the chest shaking, the blood lighting the wick, the rift, the touch on the cauldron, the soul watching the suit-up in blue fire, the Jyoti Seal on the palms, wave 4's eight soldiers, and the Warlord bursting from the boulder.
